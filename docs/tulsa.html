City pickup and drop-off rules

Bulky Waste Pick Up

Paid curb pickup: the city picks up bulky waste that does not fit in your refuse cart. Furniture, including mattresses and sofas, is accepted (Bulky Waste Pick Up).

Fee

$20 per 8 cubic yards (the city raised the fee from $10 to $20 beginning October 1, 2026). Each extra 8 cubic yards is another $20. If your pile is not compliant, the city still charges the scheduling fee and will not collect the items.

How to book

To book, schedule online at the city's Bulky Waste app (you need your City utility Customer ID and Account number), call 311 during business hours, or email Tulsa311@cityoftulsa.org. Book at least three days before your regular collection day. Pickup is on your regular refuse day.

Setting it out

Set items at the curb by 5:00 a.m. on the day. Keep them clear of power lines and trees, and do not block them with cars. Keep them off water meters, gas meters, telephone boxes and cable boxes, and at least three feet from a fence or from trash and recycling carts.

More details

Separate electronics, appliances and tires from other bulky items so the right trucks can take them.

Also accepted

Also accepted (with limits): large empty appliances; computers, monitors and TVs (limit four monitors and/or TVs); tires without rims (limit four); latex paint (up to 10 gallons); large yard waste cut to under 6 feet long and under 18 inches across; and small resident-only remodeling debris up to one cubic yard, securely containerized. No contractor debris.

Not accepted

Automobile parts, batteries, liquids, poisonous or hazardous materials, explosives, contractor remodeling or demolition debris, and loose glass. Glass that is accepted must be in a separate box or container.

Canceling

Cancel by noon the day before, or the city will still charge your account. Small items one person can handle that weigh 40 pounds or less should go in a plastic bag with an orange or purple refuse sticker for regular collection instead.

Questions

Not sure if your item or address qualifies? Check with the city by calling 311 or emailing Tulsa311@cityoftulsa.org.

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Other options

Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Hire a hauler

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
