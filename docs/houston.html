How to get rid of a mattress or couch in Houston, TX

Step 1: Can someone else use it? Donate first

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Step 2: City pickup and drop-off rules

  • Heavy trash is picked up every other month, in even-numbered months (Feb, Apr, Jun, Aug, Oct, Dec), for occupied homes with city curbside service. Buildings with more than 8 units are not served.
  • Accepted: household furniture (couches, tables, dressers), mattresses and box springs, carpet, toilets, and some appliances. Refrigerators and freezers need a certified technician's tag showing the refrigerant was removed.
  • Limit: 8 cubic yards per set-out (about the size of a minivan). Piles that mix allowed and not-allowed items may be refused.
  • Set out between 6 p.m. the Friday before and 7 a.m. on your collection day, just behind the curb, not in the street or on the sidewalk.
  • Between pickup months, you can use a city Residential Drop-Off location (bring proof of address).

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Official source: Houston Solid Waste Management
Last checked: 2026-10-25

Step 3: Other options

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
