City pickup and drop-off rules

Bulky waste pickup

The City of New Orleans picks up bulky waste from eligible homes. Bulky waste means items too big or heavy for your trash cart, such as sofas, mattresses, furniture, and appliances (Trash Collection).

How to book

To get a couch or mattress picked up, you must contact 311 to request a bulky waste pickup. Call 311 or 504-539-3266, or go to NOLA 311 (Trash Collection).

Setting it out

Do not put bulky items at the curb more than 24 hours before your scheduled trash day. The city says residents who put them out earlier will be fined. You can look up your trash day by address on the city's Trash & Recycling Collection Schedule page.

Who is served

City trash pickup is for homes with four units or less under one roof, and for small businesses. Small businesses do not get bulky waste pickup and must hire a private company. Hotels, bars, restaurants, and buildings with five or more units must hire private trash pickup.

More details

The French Quarter and Downtown Development District follow separate rules and schedules (Trash Collection).

Your trash company

The city is split into five service areas. IV Waste serves areas 1, 2, and 5, Waste Pro serves area 3, and Richard's Disposal serves area 4. Your area and pickup days are on the collection schedule page.

More details

For a missed pickup or any trash question, call 311 (Trash Collection).

Holiday weeks

On Thanksgiving Day, New Year's Day, Mardi Gras Day, and Christmas Day, pickups outside the French Quarter and Downtown Development District move forward one day. For example, Thursday pickups move to Friday, and Friday pickups move to Saturday (Collection Schedule).

Big cleanouts

For big cleanouts, the city lists two landfills that take construction and demolition debris and other materials for a fee: Gentilly C&D Landfill, 10200 Almonaster Blvd, New Orleans, LA 70127, 504-240-0380, and RiverBirch Landfill, 2000 S. Kenner Ave, Avondale, LA 70094, 504-436-1288. Call first to ask whether they take a mattress or couch, and what it costs (Trash Collection).

Illegal dumping

Leaving furniture, appliances, or trash on a street, on city property, or on private property without the owner's consent is illegal dumping. If you see someone dumping, call the NOPD non-emergency line at 504-821-2222. Report dumping you find later to 311 (Illegal Dumping).

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Other options

Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Hire a hauler

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.

Find your hauler

IV Waste serves areas 1, 2, and 5.

Waste Pro serves area 3.

Richard's Disposal serves area 4.

Your area and pickup days are on the city's collection schedule page.
