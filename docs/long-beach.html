How to get rid of a mattress or couch in Long Beach, CA

Buying a new mattress? If a store delivers a new mattress to you in California, state law says the store must offer to pick up your old one at no extra cost. If it came by a shipping company, the store must offer to arrange pickup within 30 days. Stores may refuse a contaminated mattress (CalRecycle; Cal. Public Resources Code 42992).

City pickup and drop-off rules

Special Collections

Free curb pickup: the city gives each home with City of Long Beach trash service 12 Special Collections per calendar year at no extra cost. Furniture, such as a couch, and other oversized items qualify. A fee may apply for items that need special handling (Special Collection 101).

How to book

To book, use the city's online request form or call the Call Center at 562-570-2876. You are not scheduled until the Call Center confirms your date by phone or email (Request a Special Collection).

What fits in one pickup

Each pickup takes up to 8 large items or 25 bags, bundles or boxes, and you may use two free pickups on the same day. Items should be no bigger than 72 by 48 inches. If something is bigger, like a sofa, tell the city when you book.

Mattresses and set-out time

Mattresses: do not put anything on top of a mattress you set out. Put items at your regular collection spot by 6 a.m. on your confirmed day. Crews cannot go onto private property.

More details

The city's two pages word the earliest set-out time a little differently (one says not before your day, the other says not until the evening before), so check with the Call Center if you are unsure.

Appliances, TVs and tires

Appliances, TVs, computer monitors and tires go on a separate truck. Keep them apart from your other items, and expect a possible fee.

24/7 mattress drop-off

Free 24/7 mattress drop-off: Long Beach residents can leave old mattresses and box springs any time, day or night, in the marked spot at 970 W. Chester Place. It costs nothing and needs no forms. Only mattresses and box springs are taken, it is for Long Beach residents only, and no staff are there to help you unload (24/7 Mattress Drop-Off).

Illegal dumping

See a couch or mattress dumped on the street? Report it with the city's online form or call the Refuse Hotline at 562-570-2876 (Report Illegal Dumping).

Questions

Not sure if your home has city trash service or if an item qualifies? Check with the city by calling 562-570-2876.

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Still in good shape? Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.
  • If a store delivers a new mattress to you in California, state law says the store must offer to pick up your old one at no extra cost. If it came by a shipping company, the store must offer to arrange pickup within 30 days. Stores may refuse a contaminated mattress (CalRecycle; Cal. Public Resources Code 42992). You can also drop off a mattress for free at Bye Bye Mattress sites: byebyemattress.com/california.

Hiring a hauler instead

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
