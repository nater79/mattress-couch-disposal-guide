Step 1: Can someone else use it? Give it away first

  • Offer good items free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, or shelter and ask if they take it and if they pick up. Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Step 2: Springfield rules and drop-off sites

  • No city curbside junk pickup. Trash in Springfield is picked up by more than 10 private, licensed hauling companies. Ask your own hauler about bulky items. (City of Springfield)
  • Mattresses and box springs: $20 per piece, any size. They are recycled (through a contract with Victory Mission), not buried. They must be clean, dry, and free of bugs. Wet or heavily soiled ones are refused. (Mattress Recycling page)
  • Mattress drop-off sites: (same page)
    • Lone Pine Avenue Recycling Center, 3020 S. Lone Pine Ave. Tuesday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
    • Franklin Avenue Recycling Center, 731 N. Franklin Ave. Tuesday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
    • Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill, 3545 W. Farm Road 34, Willard. Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; Saturday 7 a.m.–2:30 p.m.
  • Couches, furniture, and other junk: the Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill charges $43.85 per ton, with a $20 minimum per vehicle. It takes cash, check, and cards. (Landfill page) Small loads you unload by hand go to the Small Load Drop-off. Phone 417-742-3536. (Landfill guide)
  • Recycling centers (cans, cardboard, glass, paper, plastic, yard waste) are free, with donations welcome. They are generally open Tuesday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Franklin Avenue and Lone Pine take only cash or check. (Recycling Centers page)

Step 3: Ozark rules

  • Ozark lets you pick any trash company. The city partners with Republic Services and puts that bill on your water bill. Republic customer service: 800-627-1717. (City of Ozark)
  • Republic Services offers bulk pickup in some areas. It asks you to wrap mattresses in plastic, leave sofas and chairs in one piece, and take doors off fridges and washers. Ask them if it is offered at your address and what it costs. (Republic Services)
  • Do not leave junk in the yard. Ozark's code says furniture, appliances, and other junk must be properly disposed of. Questions: 417-581-2407. (Ozark Code Compliance)

We could not find a current Ozark citywide cleanup date on an official page. Please call the city to ask.
