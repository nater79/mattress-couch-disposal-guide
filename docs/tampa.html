City pickup and drop-off rules

S.W.E.E.P. yearly pickup

Free once a year: homes with an active City of Tampa solid waste account get one prescheduled S.W.E.E.P. (bulky item) collection every year at no additional cost. Furniture and mattresses are accepted.

More details

This is for single-family homes and properties with four units or less (S.W.E.E.P.).

Find your date

Your date is set by the city. Look it up by entering your address on the city's S.W.E.E.P. page.

Setting it out

Put everything together in one pile, at the curb on the right of way in front of your home, no later than 8 p.m. on your Sunday S.W.E.E.P. clean-up date. The earliest you can set items out is five days before that Sunday. Crews will not come back later in the week.

Pile size

Keep the pile to 10 cubic yards or less (1 cubic yard is about the size of an oven). Bigger piles may cost extra. Keep it away from low tree limbs, power or phone lines, water meters and utility boxes, and do not let cars block it.

More details

Tape or wrap mirrors and glass tabletops.

Not accepted

Building materials, electronics, household hazardous waste, pressurized gas cylinders, explosives or ammunition, metal drums, boats, and loose yard waste. Items left in front of vacant homes or lots are treated as illegal dumping.

Special pickups

Need it gone sooner? Call Solid Waste Customer Service at (813) 274-8811 to schedule a special pickup for $18.12 per cubic yard. The charge goes on your City of Tampa utility bill (Solid Waste FAQs).

McKay Bay Scale House

Haul it yourself for free: residents with an active residential solid waste account can drop off furniture and mattresses at no additional charge at the McKay Bay Scale House, 114 South 34th Street. Bring a driver's license or other government ID and a City of Tampa utility bill in the same name, and the items must come from that address. Closed-toed shoes are required.

More details

Hours: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.; Wednesday and Saturday 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.; closed Sunday. Phone: (813) 242-5320 (McKay Bay Scale House).

New Tampa and outside the city

New Tampa does not get S.W.E.E.P. because Hillsborough County Solid Waste serves it. New Tampa residents and anyone outside Tampa city limits should call Hillsborough County Solid Waste at (813) 272-5680.

Questions

Not sure if your home or item qualifies? Check with the city by calling (813) 274-8811.

Other options

Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Hire a hauler

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.

Free drop-off for residents

McKay Bay Scale House, 114 South 34th Street, Tampa, FL

Bring ID and a City of Tampa utility bill in the same name.
