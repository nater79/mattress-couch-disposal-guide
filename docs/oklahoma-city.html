How to get rid of a mattress or couch in Oklahoma City, OK

Official sourceCity of OKC Utilities
Last checked2026-10-25
StatusVerified on official page

Step 1: Can someone else use it? Donate first

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Step 2: City pickup and drop-off rules

  • Bulky waste is picked up once a month for single homes. Find your day at the city's MyTrashDay page.
  • Furniture is accepted. The first 4 cubic yards are included in your bill; more costs extra.
  • Set items out loose, by 6 a.m. on your day, but no more than 3 days early. Leave 5 feet clear on all sides.
  • Mattresses with bed bugs or biohazards must be wrapped in plastic and labeled 'bed bugs' or 'biohazard'.

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Step 3: Other options

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
