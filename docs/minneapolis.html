MN · Mattress & couch disposal

How to get rid of a mattress or couch in Minneapolis, MN

Source: City of Minneapolis Solid Waste & Recycling · Last checked 2026-11-16 · ● Verified on official page

City pickup and drop-off rules

Weekly large item pickup

If you have City of Minneapolis garbage service, crews will take up to two large items each week as part of your garbage collection. Some items can only be set out on your recycling week (Large Item Collection). The city page does not list a separate fee for large items; Solid Waste & Recycling pickup fees are on your monthly City of Minneapolis utility bill (Solid Waste & Recycling).

Couches: garbage day

Put a couch out on your garbage day. It must be less than six feet in length. Hide-a-beds and recliners are accepted on recycling week only.

More details

Wood or upholstered furniture also goes out on garbage day. Do not take furniture apart: if it is broken down, the crew may tag it as building materials, which the city does not pick up (Large Item Collection).

Mattresses: recycling day

Mattresses and box springs go out on your recycling day. Crews put a “We’ll be back” sticker on the item and come back the next business day, so leave it out with the sign and sticker on until it is picked up. Keep the mattress as dry as you can.

More details

Waterlogged or frozen mattresses are too heavy and cannot be collected for recycling (Large Item Collection).

Setting it out

Large items must be out by 6 a.m. on your pickup day, at the alley or curb line (Collection Issues). Attach a note to each item that says “For Solid Waste.” If items are not marked, the city may not collect them. Find your pickup day with the city's lookup tool (Large Item Collection).

No City service?

You do not have City collection service if you have dumpsters or carts from a private hauler. In that case, the city points you to Hennepin County's Green Disposal Guide (Large Item Collection). Check with your hauler about bulky pickup.

South Transfer Station

With City garbage service you get up to six cleanup vouchers a year for the South Transfer Station, 2850 20th Ave S. Each voucher covers up to 2,000 pounds of household garbage and up to two large recyclable items, such as mattresses. The station is open Tuesday to Saturday, but you must call 612-673-2917 to request a voucher before you go; you cannot get one on-site (Vouchers for garbage drop-off).

County mattress drop-off

Hennepin County takes household mattresses and box springs at its Brooklyn Park drop-off facility, 8100 Jefferson Highway, for $25 each piece. It does not take sleeper sofas, or soiled or bedbug infested mattresses (Mattresses and box springs). The facilities are for Hennepin County households only and are open Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Bring proof of residency.

More details

Questions: 612-348-3777 (Drop-off facilities).

Missed pickup and questions

Missed pickup? Call Solid Waste & Recycling at 612-673-2917 the following business day; you must report it within two business days. Office hours are 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday.

More details

You can also email SWRcustomer@minneapolismn.gov (Large Item Collection).

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Other options

Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Hennepin County says mattresses in good condition can be sold or given away, or donated to Bridging (Mattresses and box springs). Call first to ask what they accept.

Hire a hauler

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
