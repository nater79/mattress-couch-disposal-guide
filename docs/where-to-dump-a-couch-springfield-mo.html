Where to dump a couch in Springfield, MO

The main public place to take an old couch is the Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill near Willard. Before you haul it, see if someone can still use it.

SourcesCity of Springfield, Republic Services
Last checked2026-10-26
StatusChecked on official pages

Option 1: Give it away

If it is clean and in good shape, offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace). Or call a thrift store or church and ask if they take couches and if they pick up.

Option 2: Take it to the landfill

  • Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill, 3545 W. Farm Road 34, Willard. Phone 417-742-3536. (Landfill page)
  • Fee: $43.85 per ton, with a $20 minimum per vehicle. One couch usually weighs far less than a ton, so expect to pay the minimum, but the scale decides. (Landfill page)
  • Pay with cash, check, or a card.
  • Call ahead to check hours before you go.

Option 3: Ask your hauler for bulk pickup

Springfield's trash is handled by more than 10 private haulers. (City of Springfield) Ask yours if they pick up furniture and what it costs. Republic Services asks that sofas be left in one piece. (Republic Services)

Option 4: Hire a junk removal company

Get a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally. See our Springfield and Ozark junk removal guide for company links. (Those are plain links, not affiliate links.)

Do not leave a couch on the roadside or in an empty lot. That is illegal dumping.
