City pickup and drop-off rules

Outside the Cart pickup

Twice a month, on your regular collection day, the city picks up bulk waste left outside the cart, such as furniture. This Outside the Cart collection is for City of Memphis residential customers, and your weekly garbage and recycling cart pickup stays the same (Solid Waste). A cost for this curbside bulk pickup is not stated on the city's Solid Waste or Household Garbage pages; call 311 to confirm.

Find your schedule

Look up your collection schedule by address on the city's Solid Waste page. The city asks you to call 311 so crews know your items are at the curb and ready for pickup (Household Garbage).

Setting it out

Put the pile within 3 feet of the street. Do not put it near mailboxes, parked cars, fences, trees, or low-hanging tree limbs. Do not put items at the curb after crews have passed your house (Household Garbage).

Pile size

Bulky waste piles must not be more than 10 cubic yards in total (Household Garbage). Keep tree limbs and branches in a separate pile, away from furniture, mattresses and other bulky household waste (city notice, Oct 31, 2025).

Not accepted

By city ordinance, crews will not remove debris from construction or demolition, such as dirt, brick, rock, concrete, scrap, lumber, roofing, fencing materials, or carpet (Household Garbage).

Convenience Centers

Haul it yourself: the city has two Solid Waste Convenience Centers, at 304 Collins Street, Memphis, TN 38112, and 3207 Farrisview Blvd, Memphis, TN 38118. They are open Friday, Saturday, and Sunday from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., weather permitting (Convenience Center Locations). Bring your latest MLGW bill and a picture ID (Solid Waste).

More details

The city page does not list which items each center takes, so check with the city by calling 311 before you bring a mattress or couch.

Landfill trips

Solid Waste Fee paying customers can also take up to one truckload of non-hazardous waste to the landfill each month (Solid Waste). Check with the city by calling 311 for which landfill to use.

Questions

Not sure if your home or item qualifies? Check with the city by calling 311, or 901-636-6500, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (Call 311).

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Other options

Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Hire a hauler

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
