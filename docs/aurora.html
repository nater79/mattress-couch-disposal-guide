City pickup and drop-off rules

No city bulky pickup

In Aurora, home trash and recycling pickup is not a city service. Residents, homeowners associations, or apartment building managers set up service with one of the city's licensed haulers, and every home in Aurora must have weekly trash service (Trash & Recycling). The city pages we checked do not list a city curbside pickup for mattresses or couches.

Ask your hauler

Ask your trash hauler whether it takes a mattress or couch, how to book it, and what it costs. The city lists these licensed residential haulers: Apex Waste Solutions, Best Cleaner Disposal, Eastern Dispose All, HBS Trash Services, Mountain High Disposal, Republic Services, Waste Connections, and Waste Management. The city says being on the list is not an endorsement, and not every company serves every neighborhood (Trash & Recycling).

More details

If you rent, ask your landlord or property manager who your hauler is.

Until it is picked up

City code says furniture, mattresses, and appliances may not be stored outdoors, and dumping trash on public or private property is not allowed. Trash containers may not go in the front yard or alley before 5 p.m. the day before pickup, and must be taken in within 12 hours after pickup (Neighborhood Zoning Codes).

Mattress recycling

For mattress recycling, the city points residents to Spring Back Colorado Mattress Recycling, 4975 Pontiac St., Commerce City, CO 80022, 720-515-1328. It is open Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. (Mattress Recycling). Spring Back's own site lists a $40 per piece drop-off fee and says no appointment is needed (Spring Back Colorado).

More details

Call to confirm before you go. The city pages we checked do not name a drop-off site for couches, so check with your hauler or the city.

Recycling events

The city says it holds recycling events for electronics, mattresses, and more during the year (Neighborhood Zoning Codes). Look for upcoming dates on the city's Recycling Opportunities page.

Big cleanouts

Storage PODs and dumpsters are not allowed on the public street. The city may allow one on private property, such as a driveway, through its Temporary Use Permit program (Trash & Recycling).

Questions and reports

To report a code violation, such as trash dumped on a property, call 303-739-7000 or make a report online (Code Enforcement). For pickup questions, check with your hauler first.

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Other options

Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

The city lists Habitat for Humanity (303-421-5300), Salvation Army (800-728-7825), and ARC Thrift Stores (303-238-5263 or 800-283-2721) among groups that will schedule pickup of usable furniture from your home. Check with each group for details and restrictions (Usable Furniture).

Hire a hauler

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
