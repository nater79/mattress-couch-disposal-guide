Bulk trash pickup in Springfield, MO

Short answer: the City of Springfield does not run its own trash trucks. Your private trash hauler handles pickup, so they are the ones to ask about big items. If they can't help, you can drop items off yourself.

SourcesCity of Springfield, Republic Services
Last checked2026-10-26
StatusChecked on official pages

Step 1: Call your own trash hauler

  • Trash in Springfield is picked up by more than 10 private, licensed hauling companies. You pick your own. (City of Springfield)
  • Ask your hauler: Do you pick up couches, mattresses, or appliances? What does it cost? How should I set it out?
  • Not sure who your hauler is? Check your trash bill.

Step 2: How to set items out (Republic Services example)

  • Republic Services offers bulk pickup in some areas. It asks you to wrap mattresses in plastic, leave sofas and chairs in one piece, and take the doors off fridges and washers. (Republic Services)
  • Other haulers may have different rules. Ask yours.

Step 3: Or drop it off yourself

  • Mattresses and box springs: $20 per piece at three city sites. They must be clean, dry, and free of bugs. (Mattress Recycling page)
    • Lone Pine Avenue Recycling Center, 3020 S. Lone Pine Ave. Tuesday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
    • Franklin Avenue Recycling Center, 731 N. Franklin Ave. Tuesday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
    • Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill, 3545 W. Farm Road 34, Willard. Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; Saturday 7 a.m.–2:30 p.m.
  • Couches and other junk: Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill charges $43.85 per ton, $20 minimum per vehicle. Cash, check, and cards. Phone 417-742-3536. (Landfill page)
  • Recycling questions: city Recycling Hotline 417-864-1904. (Recycling Centers page)

Still good? Give it away

Offer clean items free to neighbors, or call a thrift store or church first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Anything not listed here: check with the city or your hauler. Fees and hours change. Last checked 2026-10-26.
