Mattress recycling fee in Springfield, MO

The City of Springfield charges $20 per piece to recycle a mattress or box spring, any size. A mattress plus a box spring is $40. (Mattress Recycling page)

SourcesCity of Springfield
Last checked2026-10-26
StatusChecked on official pages

Where to take it

  • Lone Pine Avenue Recycling Center, 3020 S. Lone Pine Ave. Tuesday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
  • Franklin Avenue Recycling Center, 731 N. Franklin Ave. Tuesday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
  • Noble Hill Sanitary Landfill, 3545 W. Farm Road 34, Willard. Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; Saturday 7 a.m.–2:30 p.m.

Source: (City of Springfield Mattress Recycling)

What they will and won't take

  • Taken: mattresses and box springs.
  • Not taken: wet, bug-infested, or heavily soiled items.
  • Paying: Franklin Avenue and Lone Pine take only cash or check. The landfill also takes cards. (Recycling Centers page)

Where does it go?

The program runs through a contract with Victory Mission, a local nonprofit. Mattresses are taken apart, and the parts become raw materials for things like building materials and wood chip mulch. The city says more than 12,000 mattresses have been collected since 2018. (City of Springfield)

Tips

  • Keep it dry. Cover it if it rides in an open truck bed.
  • Live in Ozark or nearby? These are City of Springfield sites. Call the Recycling Hotline (417-864-1904) to ask if you can use them.
