How to get rid of a mattress or couch in San Diego, CA

Step 1: Can someone else use it? Donate first

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.
  • If a store delivers a new mattress to you in California, state law says the store must offer to pick up your old one at no extra cost. If it came by a shipping company, the store must offer to arrange pickup within 30 days. Stores may refuse a contaminated mattress (CalRecycle; Cal. Public Resources Code 42992). You can also drop off a mattress for free at Bye Bye Mattress sites: byebyemattress.com/california.

Step 2: City pickup and drop-off rules

  • The city does NOT offer curbside bulky item or mattress pickup.
  • Free mattress and box spring drop-off: Mattress Collection Site, 5161 Convoy St. (Miramar Landfill entrance), Mon-Fri 7 a.m.-3 p.m.
  • Mattresses must not be wet, badly damaged, soiled, or have bed bugs. Do not bring them in a box or plastic bag.
  • Other bulky items: haul to Miramar Landfill (fee) or watch for city community cleanup events.

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Official source: City of San Diego Environmental Services
Last checked: 2026-10-25

Step 3: Other options

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
