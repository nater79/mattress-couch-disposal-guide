City of Fort Worth Environmental Services

The city page does not name couches or mattresses. It covers items too large for the brown cart. Please check with the city (817-392-1234 or 1234@fortworthtexas.gov) before setting them out.

Book or read the official rules

City pickup and drop-off rules

Bulk items are picked up once a month during your assigned week, up to 10 cubic yards free. One pile per home.

Set out by 7 a.m. Monday of your week (no earlier than 6 p.m. the Friday before).

Keep the pile at least 2 feet from trees, mailboxes, meters and fences.

Wrong-time or wrong-item piles cost $123.52 per 5 cubic yards (minimum).

You can also take bulk items to a city Drop-Off Station for free.

Anything not listed here: check with the city. We only list rules we could confirm on an official page.

Official source: City of Fort Worth Environmental Services

Still in good shape? Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Hiring a hauler instead

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.
