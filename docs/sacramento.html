CA · Mattress & couch disposal

How to get rid of a mattress or couch in Sacramento, CA

Buying a new mattress? If a store delivers a new mattress to you in California, state law says the store must offer to pick up your old one at no extra cost. If it came by a shipping company, the store must offer to arrange pickup within 30 days. Stores may refuse a contaminated mattress (CalRecycle; Cal. Public Resources Code 42992). Or drop it off free at a Bye Bye Mattress site.

City pickup and drop-off rules

Household Junk Pickup

Free curb pickup: every home with City of Sacramento trash service gets two free Household Junk Pickup appointments per calendar year. Furniture, such as a couch, and mattresses are accepted. Free appointments run February through October, and the city stops taking them in mid- to late-October for leaf season (Household Junk Pickup).

How to book

To book, send a service request online through Sac311, use the Sac311 app, or call 311 (916-264-5011 from outside city limits). Name the items you plan to set out. Tenants of single-family homes and duplexes can book too.

Setting it out

Put items on the public street no earlier than 24 hours before your appointment, and by 6:00 a.m. on the day. Early set-outs may be fined. Do not put the pile in an alley or driveway, do not block the sidewalk or street, and keep it 6 feet away from parked cars.

Pile size and what is not taken

The pile can be no bigger than 4 by 4 by 9 feet, and bigger piles may cost extra. Put loose small items in cardboard boxes. Appliances and e-waste are not part of this pickup; book a separate appointment for them (Household Junk Pickup Guidelines).

Canceling

To cancel or move your date, do it by 5:00 p.m. two business days before. If you do not, the city counts it as one of your free pickups.

Paid pickups and self-haul

From November through January, or once your two free pickups are used, you may be able to book a paid pickup instead. Call City Utility Billing at 916-808-5454. The minimum fee is $39.48.

More details

The city's guidelines also say you can call 311 to ask for a free dump coupon and haul the items yourself to the Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station, 8491 Fruitridge Road (city facility page). Check with the city that your mattress or couch is taken there before you go.

Apartments (5+ units)

Apartments with 5 or more units that do not get City of Sacramento trash service: ask your landlord, property manager or commercial hauler to set up a junk pickup.

Illegal dumping

See a couch or mattress dumped on the street? Report it online, with the Sac311 app, or by calling 311 or 916-264-5011 (Illegal Dumping).

Questions

Not sure if your home has city service or if an item qualifies? Check with the city by calling 311 (916-264-5011).

Other options

Donate it

If your couch or mattress is clean and in good shape, giving it away helps a neighbor and keeps it out of the landfill.

  • Offer it free to neighbors (Buy Nothing groups, Nextdoor, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist "free").
  • Call a local thrift store, church, shelter, or furniture bank. Ask if they take it and if they pick up.
  • Call first. Many charities do not take used mattresses.

Hire a hauler

If the city cannot help, you can hire a licensed hauler. Ask for a written price first. Never pay someone who might dump it illegally.

Self-haul with a dump coupon

Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station, 8491 Fruitridge Road

Ask 311 for a free dump coupon first, and check that your item is taken.
