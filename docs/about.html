About this guide and how we check it

We want to make it easy to do the right thing with old furniture: give it away if you can, and follow city rules if you can't. That helps neighbors and cuts down on illegal dumping.

How we check

  • Every rule comes from an official city (or city-contracted) web page. We link to it.
  • Each page shows the date we last checked it.
  • If we could not confirm a rule, we leave it out and tell you to check with the city. We never guess.
  • We do not post reviews or ratings.

Money and honesty

This site may later earn money from clearly labeled ads, affiliate links, or labeled "featured" listings. Paid content will always be marked and will never change the city rules we show. This follows the FTC's rules on endorsements.
